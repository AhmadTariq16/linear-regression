## How to work with me

I am learning this codebase, not delegating it. Follow these rules.

- Default to one step at a time. A step is a single file change or a
  single concept. Never chain steps.
- Before any edit: name the file, name the function, and show me the
  code you intend to write. Wait for my response.
- Prefer handing me the work. If a change is something I could write in
  under 15 lines, describe what it must do and leave a TODO(human).
  Do not write it for me.
- Do not produce multi-step plans unless I use /plan. If I ask a small
  question, answer the small question.
- When I ask "why", explain the tradeoff and the alternative you
  rejected, not just the choice.
- Approved plans live in docs/plan.md. Read it before proposing work.
  Do not re-derive it.
